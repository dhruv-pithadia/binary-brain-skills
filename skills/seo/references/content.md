# Content and search appearance

## Titles, descriptions and structure

Give important indexable pages descriptive titles and useful descriptions through their templates and content model. Check rendered output for duplicates, placeholder text, wrong origins, escaped markup and missing data. Write for the actual page's purpose and likely intent; do not force fixed character counts or repeat target phrases mechanically. Search engines may rewrite titles and snippets.

Use a clear main heading and logical heading structure. Multiple H1 elements are not by themselves proof of an indexing failure. Prefer semantic clarity over a blanket H1-count rule. Keep meaningful content accessible without unnecessary interaction, while preserving intentional authenticated experiences.

Link text should describe its destination in context. Informative images need useful alternative text; decorative images normally need empty alt text. Describe the image or its function rather than stuffing keywords. Avoid putting essential text only in an image.

## Structured data

Choose a type only when the page actually represents that entity and its visible content supports the fields. Article, Product, Organization and BreadcrumbList are not interchangeable labels for every route. Confirm current search-feature eligibility and required properties before implementing. Use safe JSON serialization, verify URLs and data types, and test the actual emitted JSON-LD with suitable validators. Schema validity is not a guarantee of a rich result.

Do not add FAQPage or Article markup to every page. Google retired FAQ rich results in 2026; verify current engine-specific support rather than promising visibility from FAQ markup. Existing schema may still describe content for other consumers, so do not delete it indiscriminately. Keep useful visible FAQs when they answer real questions.

Never fabricate reviews, ratings, prices, inventory, credentials, authors, dates or organizational identity. Identify missing information as a dependency on the owner. Markup must not contradict what visitors see.

## Trust, usefulness and authority

Assess whether the page answers a real user question with accurate, distinctive information. Fix unclear explanations, unsupported claims and redundant generated text. AI use alone does not determine content quality; mass-producing thin pages is not a content strategy.

Use real authorship, expertise and ownership details where appropriate. Product pages and utility routes do not need a fictional author bio. Preserve legal claims and regulated content pending owner review. Support factual claims with suitable evidence and keep important content current.

Recommend useful resources, product evidence and relevant relationships for earning attention. Backlink outreach is separate authorized work. Do not buy links, create fake endorsements, post unsolicited messages, or promise a ranking outcome from a link count. Keyword research and content expansion should use available evidence, not invented search volumes or competitor metrics.
